About the Role

Applied Materials is seeking a Quality Engineer to join our Semiconductor Systems division in Santa Clara, CA. In this role, you will drive quality improvements across our wafer inspection and metrology product lines, working closely with process engineers, manufacturing teams, and customers to ensure world-class product quality.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead quality engineering activities for wafer inspection and thin film metrology systems
  • Develop and implement Statistical Process Control (SPC) programs to monitor critical parameters
  • Apply Lean Six Sigma methodology to drive continuous improvement initiatives across manufacturing processes
  • Ensure compliance with GMP, ISO 9001, and semiconductor industry standards
  • Perform root cause analysis using FMEA, 8D, and Fishbone methodologies
  • Manage clean room qualification protocols and support photolithography process controls
  • Collaborate with suppliers on incoming quality and corrective action programs
  • Support customer escalations and coordinate field quality issues
  • Create and maintain quality control plans, inspection procedures, and measurement system analyses

Required Qualifications

  • BS/MS in Engineering, Physics, Materials Science, or related technical field
  • 3+ years of quality engineering experience in semiconductor equipment or related industry
  • Strong knowledge of SPC, measurement system analysis, and statistical tools
  • Experience with clean room environments and semiconductor fabrication processes
  • GMP compliance experience (ISO 13485 or ISO 9001)
  • Six Sigma Green Belt or Black Belt certification preferred
  • Familiarity with wafer metrology and inspection techniques (optical, SEM, AFM)
  • Excellent problem-solving skills with demonstrated FMEA and 8D experience

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with EUV or advanced lithography systems
  • Python or data analysis scripting for quality data automation
  • Background in thin film deposition or etch process quality
  • Familiarity with APC (Advanced Process Control) systems

  • Comprehensive benefits including medical, dental, vision
  • RSU equity grants and annual bonus program
  • Visa sponsorship: H-1B and TN Visa eligible
  • Relocation assistance available
